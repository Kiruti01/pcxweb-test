## Introduction

PCX PAY Limited ("PCXPAY", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use the PCXPAY platform, API, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

PCX PAY Limited is an appointed agent of Lightway Finance Limited. This Privacy Policy applies to all users of our Services, including Business Users, Consumer Retail End-Users, Developer Users, and End-Users as defined in our [Terms and Conditions](/terms_and_conditions).

## Data We Collect

### Identity and Verification Data

- Full legal name, date of birth, and nationality
- Government-issued identification documents (passport, national ID, driving licence)
- Proof of address documentation
- Business registration documents and beneficial ownership information (Business and Developer Users)
- Biometric verification data where required by applicable law

### Contact and Account Data

- Email address, phone number, and postal address
- Account credentials and login history
- Communication and notification preferences

### Transaction Data

- Transaction history, amounts, currencies, and timestamps
- Digital wallet addresses and payment references
- Counterparty information associated with transfers

### Technical and Usage Data

- IP addresses, device identifiers, browser type and version
- API access logs, request metadata, and response codes (Developer Users)
- Platform usage patterns and feature interactions
- Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Cookies section below)

### Compliance and Risk Data

- KYC/AML verification outcomes and documentation
- Sanctions screening and Politically Exposed Person (PEP) check results
- Risk assessment scores and transaction monitoring flags

## How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

- **Service delivery** — To provide, maintain, and improve the PCXPAY Services and process your transactions
- **Identity verification** — To conduct KYC/AML checks as required by applicable law in the UK, Nigeria, and Canada
- **Legal compliance** — To meet our obligations under FCA regulations, CBN guidelines, FINTRAC requirements, UK GDPR, NDPR, and PIPEDA
- **Fraud prevention and security** — To detect, prevent, and investigate fraudulent activity, money laundering, and other prohibited activities
- **Customer support** — To respond to your enquiries and assist with dispute resolution
- **Service communications** — To send transaction confirmations, compliance notifications, and service updates
- **Marketing** — Where you have provided consent, to send information about new features and services
- **Platform analytics** — To understand how our Services are used and to improve user experience

## Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

- **Contract performance** — Processing necessary to deliver the Services you have requested
- **Legal obligation** — Processing required to comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements in the UK (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(c)), Nigeria (NDPR), and Canada (PIPEDA)
- **Legitimate interests** — Processing for fraud prevention, security monitoring, and platform improvement, where our interests are not overridden by your fundamental rights
- **Consent** — Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

## Data Sharing

We share your personal data only in the following circumstances:

### Lightway Finance Limited

As our principal under our agency agreement, Lightway Finance Limited receives data necessary to process payments, perform compliance screening, and fulfil their regulatory obligations as a licensed payment service provider.

### Regulatory Authorities

We disclose data as required by law to the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC), and other competent authorities with jurisdiction over our operations.

### Third-Party Service Providers

We engage trusted processors for identity verification, sanctions screening, cloud infrastructure, and analytics. All processors are bound by contractual obligations to handle your data securely and only for the purposes we specify.

### Developer Users

If you access PCXPAY Services through a Developer User's platform, that Developer User receives data about your transactions and account status as necessary to operate their integrated service.

### Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to equivalent privacy protections.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide the Services and meet our legal obligations:

- **Account and identity data** — Retained for the duration of your relationship with PCXPAY and for 7 years after account closure, as required under anti-money laundering regulations
- **Transaction records** — Retained for a minimum of 7 years in the UK and Canada, and as required under Nigerian law
- **KYC/AML documentation** — Retained in accordance with FCA, CBN, and FINTRAC requirements
- **Communication records** — Retained for 3 years after last contact
- **Technical logs** — Retained for up to 12 months for security and operational purposes

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data.

### UK Users (UK GDPR)

- Right of access to your personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"), subject to our legal retention obligations
- Right to restriction of processing in certain circumstances
- Right to data portability in a machine-readable format
- Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Right to withdraw consent at any time

### Nigerian Users (NDPR)

- Right to access your personal data held by us
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to deletion, subject to legal retention obligations
- Right to object to processing of your data

### Canadian Users (PIPEDA)

- Right to access your personal data
- Right to challenge accuracy and request correction
- Right to withdraw consent, subject to legal and contractual restrictions

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [info@pcxpay.com](mailto:info@pcxpay.com). We will respond within 30 days. We may require identity verification before actioning your request.
