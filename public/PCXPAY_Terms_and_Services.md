
## Introduction

PCPCXPAY provides a proprietary software application programming interface (the "PCXPAY API") to businesses, developers, and end-users to enable them to instantly buy and sell stablecoins and other digital assets from and to, and access related services, from PCXPAY and its Affiliates or either of their Financial Institution or Payment Service Provider partners. PCX PAY Limited is an appointed agent of Lightway Finance Limited (the "PCXPAY Services", and together with the PCXPAY API, the "PCXPAY Platform").

Users maintain, are developing or are exploring developing proprietary software applications, business operations, or consumer activities (the "User Program") to provide User-branded services or access services (the "User Services" and together with the User Program, the "User Platform") to themselves or their customers ("End-Users"). Users and PCXPAY desire to offer and provide the PCXPAY Services to End-Users by integrating the PCXPAY Platform with the User Platform in a User-branded application environment or direct access model (such offering, the "Program").


## User Category

1. Business User: A legal entity or natural person acting in a trade, business, craft or profession (see Appendix-Business-User).
2. Consumer Retail End-User: Natural persons accessing Services for personal, non-commercial purposes subject to Consumer Retail End-User terms.
3. Business-User Commercial End-User: Entities or persons accessing Services for commercial purposes subject to Business-User Commercial End-User terms.
4. Developer User: Entities or persons integrating the PCXPAY API into their own platforms to offer Services to their customers.
5. End-User: Any person who accesses Services through a Developer, Partner, Sub-Developer or directly.
To support the Program, the Parties agree to the following terms.
